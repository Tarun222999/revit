# Revit app icon concepts

Created: 2026-09-06

Generated and refined with the built-in image_gen tool. These are three visual concepts for selection, not a replacement of the active application icon or a packaged set of Android adaptive layers.

## Concepts

- Revit Ribbon: `revit-icon-concept-ribbon-r.png`
- The Keepsake Ticket: `revit-icon-concept-keepsake-ticket.png`

The Story Stack was refined from blank portrait covers into broader media cards with a play cutout to reduce the reading-app association.

Sophisticated nearly flat graphic design with just one restrained plane of shading where needed to explain a fold or overlap. Strong graphic clarity at phone-launcher scale, optically centered. Main mark approximately 60% of canvas width/height with comfortable uniform breathing room. Silhouette and broad negative spaces must also remain coherent in a one-color rendering.
Composition constraints: show the actual icon artwork itself, filling the square canvas; NOT a presentation mockup, NOT a rounded app tile sitting on another background, NOT a contact sheet, NOT multiple variations. Do not pre-round the square canvas or add an outer border. No titles, captions, wordmarks, watermarks, device frames, pedestal, lighting halo, lens effects, glossy glass, bevels, noisy texture, or tiny secondary badges. Do not include an open book, pen, film reel, controller, clapperboard, calendar, sparkles, or four media-category symbols.
Subject: one bold, custom-designed uppercase R constructed from a single broad folded amber ribbon. The straight left stem, rounded upper bowl, and confident diagonal right leg must make it read clearly as R first. The interior counter of the R is a generous triangular charcoal negative space, subtly suggesting play without adding a separate play button. The bottom of the left ribbon stem ends in one broad shallow V-shaped bookmark cut. This is one unified cleverly crafted symbol. The diagonal ribbon fold has a single slightly darker amber plane that explains its construction. Use warm amber for the whole mark, with subtle lighter gold on the main face; no disconnected objects. The result should feel like a strong proprietary entertainment brand emblem, confident and inviting, with clean optical balance and a beautiful memorable silhouette.
```

#### Final refinement

The negative spaces inside and below the R must be filled with the same opaque charcoal as the background. Refined bold logo, no realistic material or 3D rendering.
```
