Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Revit handles information when you use the Revit mobile app.

Revit is a personal entertainment journal. It lets you search for movies, series, anime, and games; plan and log watches or playthroughs; rate titles; write personal notes; and organize titles into mixed-media lists.

Information Revit Stores

Revit stores the profile details you create, including your display name, username, optional bio, and avatar path.

Revit stores your personal Journal data, including saved titles, plans, watch and playthrough history, statuses, ratings, personal notes, custom lists, list items, and optional list notes. For games, this can include your played-on platform choice.

Authentication And Account Data

Revit uses Supabase Auth for sign-in. Depending on the platform and sign-in option available to you, Supabase may process your email address and identity provider information from Google or Apple.

Authentication tokens are used to keep you signed in and to make sure your private journal, lists, and profile data are only available to your account.

Journal And Lists

Your Journal lets you keep a title-level summary, an optional plan, and dated watch or playthrough history. Revit stores these records, ratings, personal notes, list names, list descriptions, and list notes so it can show and manage your private entertainment history.

Revit does not currently include public profiles, social feeds, follows, comments, or likes.

Avatars And Storage

If you upload an avatar, the image is stored in Supabase Storage and linked from your profile.

Avatar files may be publicly accessible by direct URL so they can render in the app. Your private Journal records and lists are not made public by avatar storage.

Media Metadata

Revit uses TMDB-sourced metadata for movies, series, and anime. This may include title names, images, release information, descriptions, genres, and related title details.

When Games is available, Revit uses IGDB, operated by Twitch, for public game metadata and imagery. Game search terms and public catalog requests are processed through Revit's server-side Supabase Edge Functions and may be sent to IGDB to return matching catalog results. Revit does not intentionally send your email address, profile identity, private Journal or play history, played-on platform choice, ratings, notes, or lists to IGDB.

Feedback Reports

Signed-in users can report a problem or suggest an idea through Help & Feedback in Profile. Revit stores the message you enter, the report category, whether you allow follow-up, your authenticated user ID, and the submission time so the report can be privately reviewed.

Revit also attaches limited diagnostic context when available: app version and build, platform and OS version, source screen, and a safe error code for a contextual report. It does not attach Journal text, screenshots, raw logs, request headers, passwords, one-time codes, or access tokens. Feedback reports are not public and do not automatically create a public post or a Linear issue.

How Data Is Used

Revit uses your data to provide the app experience, including authentication, profile display, search and title details, journal management, Timeline, Planner, and Calendar views, and mixed-media lists.

Revit does not sell your personal journal data.

Ads, Analytics, And Tracking

Revit does not currently include ads.

Revit does not currently include advertising, analytics, or crash reporting tools. The limited diagnostic context described above is collected only when a signed-in user chooses to submit feedback.

The public legal and support website is intended to be static and should not include tracking scripts, advertising scripts, login, forms, or database access.

Data Sharing

Revit uses service providers needed to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, database storage, file storage, and Edge Functions.

Revit uses TMDB-sourced integrations for movie, series, and anime metadata, and IGDB for game catalog metadata. Revit does not intentionally send your private Journal records, ratings, notes, or lists to either provider as part of normal catalog use.

Data Deletion

Revit supports in-app account deletion from the Profile account surface.

When account deletion succeeds, Revit removes the live authenticated user account and connected user-owned profile, Journal, play history, list, list-item, and feedback-report database records, including private game activity stored by Revit. The deletion flow also requests removal of avatar files associated with the account before deleting the account.

This does not promise immediate removal of every storage-object version or backup copy. Backup retention is managed by service providers and its applicable duration has not yet been confirmed for public release. Revit does not send private game-Journal data to IGDB for deletion because it is not sent there as part of normal catalog use. For account or data deletion questions, contact mt790191@gmail.com.

Children

Revit is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Revit changes. The Last updated date will be changed when this page is updated.
